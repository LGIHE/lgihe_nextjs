# Application Document Requirements

This document lists all the required documents that applicants need to submit after completing the online application form.

## Required Documents for All Applicants

### 1. Application Fee Payment Slip ⚠️ MANDATORY
- **Amount:** UGX 50,000
- **Payment Method:** Bank deposit or transfer
- **Bank Details:**
  - Bank: Bank Of Africa
  - Account Name: Luigi Giussani Institute of Higher Education
  - Account Number: 9133750003
  - Branch: Luzira
- **Important:** Keep the original payment slip for submission

### 2. Academic Certificates
- **O-Level Certificate** (certified copy)
  - Must show all subjects and grades
  - Certification by a Commissioner of Oaths or authorized official
  
- **A-Level Certificate** (certified copy)
  - Must show all subjects and points
  - Certification by a Commissioner of Oaths or authorized official

### 3. Identification Documents
- **National ID or Passport** (photocopy)
  - Must be valid and clearly legible
  - Both sides if National ID

### 4. Photographs
- **Two recent passport-size photographs**
  - Colored photographs
  - White or light blue background
  - Taken within the last 6 months

### 5. Birth Certificate
- **Certified copy of birth certificate**
  - Certification by a Commissioner of Oaths or authorized official

## Additional Documents (If Applicable)

### For Applicants with Other Qualifications
- Certified copies of all additional certificates, diplomas, or degrees mentioned in the application
- Transcripts if available

### For Employed Applicants
- **Letter from Current Employer**
  - On company letterhead
  - Confirming position and employment dates
  - Signed and stamped by HR or supervisor

### For International Applicants
- **Passport** (copy of bio-data page)
- **Equivalence Certificate** from Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB)
  - For foreign academic qualifications
- **Student Visa** or proof of eligibility to study in Uganda

### For Applicants with Disabilities
- **Medical Report** (if requesting special accommodations)
  - From a registered medical practitioner
  - Detailing the nature of disability and required accommodations

## Document Certification

### Who Can Certify Documents?

Documents can be certified by:
- Commissioner of Oaths
- Magistrate
- Lawyer
- Bank Manager
- Police Officer (Inspector or above)
- Religious Leader (with official stamp)
- School Principal or Headteacher

### Certification Requirements

Each certified copy must:
1. Be a clear photocopy of the original
2. Have the certifier's signature
3. Have the certifier's official stamp
4. Include the date of certification
5. Include the certifier's contact information

### Certification Statement Example

```
"I certify that this is a true copy of the original document 
which I have seen and verified.

Signature: _______________
Name: _______________
Title: _______________
Stamp: [OFFICIAL STAMP]
Date: _______________
Contact: _______________"
```

## Important Notes

### Document Quality
- All documents must be clear and legible
- Faded or unclear copies will not be accepted
- Use A4 size paper for all copies

### Original Documents
- Bring original documents for verification during in-person submission
- Originals will be returned after verification
- Do not send original documents by post

### Incomplete Applications
- Applications with missing documents will not be processed
- You will be contacted to submit missing documents
- Processing will only begin when all documents are received

### Document Retention
- Keep copies of all submitted documents for your records
- Documents submitted will not be returned
- Unsuccessful applicants can collect documents after 6 months

## Timeline

### After Document Submission
1. **Acknowledgment:** Within 3 working days
2. **Document Verification:** 5-7 working days
3. **Application Review:** 2-3 weeks
4. **Admission Decision:** Communicated via email and phone

### What Happens Next?
1. Documents are received and logged
2. Verification of authenticity
3. Academic qualifications assessment
4. Admissions committee review
5. Decision communicated to applicant
6. Successful applicants receive admission letter

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I submit documents before paying the application fee?
A: No, the application fee payment slip must be included with your document submission.

### Q: How long does document verification take?
A: Typically 5-7 working days after submission.

### Q: Can someone else submit documents on my behalf?
A: Yes, but they must carry an authorization letter from you and a copy of your ID.

### Q: What if I don't have all documents ready?
A: Submit what you have and inform the admissions office about missing documents. However, your application will only be processed when all documents are received.

### Q: Are scanned documents accepted?
A: For initial submission, yes. However, certified hard copies must be submitted before admission.

### Q: Can I track my document submission?
A: Yes, contact the admissions office with your application reference number.
